Connect the Naphotep TikTok account so finished episodes can be sent to its TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is ever published automatically: every video is reviewed and posted inside TikTok.

This app asks TikTok for:

  • user.info.basic: to confirm which account is connected
  • video.upload: to send finished videos to that account's inbox as drafts
