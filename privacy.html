Privacy Policy

Naphotep Publisher · last updated 15 November 2026 · Home

Naphotep Publisher is a private tool used by the creator of the Naphotep series to publish the series' own animated videos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool uses

When the account owner connects TikTok, TikTok provides an access token, a refresh token and a basic account identifier (open_id). These are used only to upload videos to that same account and to check whether an upload succeeded.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private repository controlled by the account owner. No other personal data is collected, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the stored secrets removes all tokens.

Contact

Send a direct message to the Naphotep account on TikTok.
